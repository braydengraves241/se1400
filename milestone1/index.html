Website Summary

What content will the site contain?

A book collection/reading tracker that I'm developing as my CS 1400 final.

Where will you get all of the content for your site (ie. images & text)?

I will be developing a lot of it myself, but the images will be uploaded by the user

Why should users care about the focus of the site?

My hope is for the site to be a good place to keep track of a user's book collection, as well as what they've read.

Why should a site be built for this?

So that readers can easily see the data on the books they own, such as the gernre or author, and potentially branch out or search for other books they may enjoy.

What would you like users to do at your site?

Track their books, add tags to them, and see all the data.

What is the goal of the site?

To help readers explore their interests, or perhaps find new ones.
